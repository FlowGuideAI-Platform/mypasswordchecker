Questions people actually ask

How long would it take to crack my password?

Depends on length, character variety and the attacker's hardware. A rented GPU rig tries roughly 100 billion guesses a second offline. Eight lowercase letters fall in seconds; thirteen mixed characters take millions of years.

What makes a password quantum resistant?

Grover's algorithm halves effective strength — 80 classical bits become 40. To hold 80 bits against a quantum attacker you need about 160 bits classically: roughly 25 random characters, or six random words.

Is this safe to use?

Your password never leaves the browser. Disconnect from the internet and the checker still works — that is the test we would run too.

What is entropy?

Randomness measured in bits. Each bit doubles the number of possibilities. Forty bits is a trillion guesses; sixty is a quintillion.
